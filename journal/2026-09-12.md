## Reasoning

A small NASDAQ down-week (**−0.66%**) met a **larger book decline (−1.02%, −$1,037)** — a **−0.36pp lag on a down-week.** That is unusual: the book's demonstrated edge is *down-week protection*, and this is the first week since inception the since-inception lead compressed on a **down** move rather than an up-week give-back. The mechanism is worth dissecting.

**All three risk drivers fell together this week.** Equities down (VOO −0.77%, VXUS −1.44%, AVUV −1.91%), bonds down (BND −1.01%), gold down (IAU −2.01%). Diversification cushions when the diversifiers zig while equities zag; this week everything zagged at once, so there was nothing to cushion with. Worse, the two highest-beta equity sleeves (small-cap value, international) and gold all fell *more* than both the S&P core and the index — so the book's blend underperformed a NASDAQ that was carried by its mega-cap weighting holding up better than the broad/factor/ex-US exposures the book tilts toward.

This is the **down-side mirror of the small-up-week misses (08-28, 09-04).** The refined, symmetric read now on tape in both directions:

- **The protection is a BIG-down-week phenomenon.** All five >2% NDX drops were cushioned (5/5) — unchanged, none this week.
- **On *small* moves (either direction), the book lags when the diversifiers move against it.** Small-up-weeks: 3/5 (misses 08-28, 09-04). Small/flat-down-weeks: **0/2** — 05-15 (NDX −0.08%, book −0.91%, −0.82pp) and now 09-11 (−0.36pp). The edge lives in the tails, not the middle.
