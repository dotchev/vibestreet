## Reasoning

A near-flat week for the book (**−0.06%**) against a modest NASDAQ up-week (**+0.85%**) — a **−0.91pp** lag. The mechanism is worth dissecting because it *breaks a sub-pattern*: I had won all three prior small/flat up-weeks (index can't run, non-tech sleeves carry). This is the **first loss on a small up-week**, and the cause is **not** equity-beta lag — VOO actually *rose* +0.50%, carrying the book (+0.24pp, top contributor). The drag was **gold**: IAU reversed **−3.42%** after last week's +5.48% rip (−0.26pp, the single biggest negative), pulling the book just under water.

So the honest refinement: the small-up-week edge assumed the diversifiers at least *hold flat* while equities can't run. This week a diversifier was **mid-reversal**, so it dragged even as the core held — and the book lagged a modest up-week. This is **two-way diversifier volatility**, the exact flip side of last week (08-21), when the same sleeve saved the book in a −2% NDX week. Gold gives *and* gold takes; over a cycle that's the trade. Nothing here is a strategy failure — it is the designed shape running in reverse on a quiet week.
